CWMS Focus Companion — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

CWMS Focus Companion is an internal browser extension used by staff alongside the CWMS Web Portal (a private time-tracking application). This policy explains what the extension accesses and, more importantly, what it does not do.

What the extension accesses

The extension uses Chrome's idle API to read whether the computer is active or idle at the operating-system level (i.e. whether the keyboard and mouse have been used recently). It does not read what you type, which applications you run, your screen contents, your browsing history, or the content of any web page.

How that information is used

The active/idle signal is passed only to the CWMS Web Portal page open in your browser, using an in-browser message (postMessage). The portal uses it so its "are you still working?" inactivity prompt is not shown while you are genuinely working in another program, and the extension brings the CWMS tab to the front when that prompt does appear. The signal is used in the moment and is not retained by the extension.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, store, or log any personal information.
  • We do not transmit any data to the extension's authors or to any third party or external server.
  • We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • We do not sell or share any data. There is no data to sell.

Permissions

  • idle — to detect OS-level active/idle state, as described above.
  • Access to the CWMS Web Portal pages only — so the extension can relay the signal to the portal and focus its tab. It runs on no other websites.

Data retention

None. The extension keeps no records.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's behaviour changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised.

Contact

Questions about this extension can be raised with your CWMS administrator, or via the project repository at github.com/augustopellis/CWMS-Web.

CWMS Focus Companion is a private, internal tool and is not affiliated with Google or Microsoft.
